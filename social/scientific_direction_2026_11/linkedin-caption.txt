We made our traffic research more sophisticated. But were we getting closer to the central question?

Our early cooperative routing policies did not establish an advantage. Better predictions did not automatically produce better collective decisions. And individual fairness remained a serious problem.

So we’re refocusing: before building another prediction engine or selecting another simulator, we want a cleaner investigation of whether beneficial, voluntary cooperation can create value beyond equally informed independent choices.

That is a change in method, not a claim of success. We’re preserving the negative and inconclusive results. No new experiment has been authorized.
